Project Overview

Live the Westin life, every day, at home

The Westin Residences is Whiteland Corporation's branded residential development with Marriott International. Its homes come with Westin hospitality and wellness services. The towers are designed by Hafeez Contractor and use biophilic planning. They sit on the Dwarka Expressway, minutes from IGI Airport and Gurugram's business districts.

  • Residences managed by Marriott International, with Westin wellness and concierge services
  • Architecture by Hafeez Contractor, with biophilic design throughout
  • High-rise towers with panoramic city views; Vastu-compliant layouts
  • Multi-dome clubhouse, spa and wellness pools, and landscaped gardens
  • 5-tier security and 3-level basement parking

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is developing The Westin Residences, Gurugram?

Whiteland Corporation Pvt. Ltd. is developing the project in association with Marriott International. The Westin Residences are managed under the Westin brand.

Where exactly is the project located?

Sector 103, Gurugram, on the Dwarka Expressway. That's about 5 minutes from the expressway and about 15 minutes from IGI Airport.

What configurations and sizes are available?

3 BHK + utility (2,673 and 2,939 sq.ft) and 4 BHK + utility (3,750, 3,962 and 4,328 sq.ft) saleable area.

What is the starting price?

Indicative pricing starts from ₹ 6.28 Cr* for a 3 BHK + utility. Prices depend on size, tower and floor. Request the latest cost sheet for exact figures.

What is the payment plan?

A construction-linked plan: 9% on booking, then 11%, 10% and 5% within 45, 75 and 120 days. After that come 15% at the 20th floor slab, 15% at super structure, 25% on application of OC and 10% on possession.

When is possession expected?

Possession is expected around May 2030, according to current developer timelines. The final date is governed by the RERA registration.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. The developer lists Haryana RERA registration nos. 65, 66 & 67 dated 18/06/2024 and 52 & 53 dated 16/05/2025 (registered project name: "Urban Resort"). You can verify them on haryanarera.gov.in.
